Reports Maven and Gradle dependencies whose publisher moved them to new coordinates.

A move comes from a <relocation> in the newest POM, for example mysql:mysql-connector-java to com.mysql:mysql-connector-j. Jackson 3 has no relocation POMs, so a com.fasterxml.jackson artifact is reported when the same artifact has a release under tools.jackson.

The old coordinates may still get releases, as Jackson 2 does. Turn this inspection off if you stay on them on purpose.
