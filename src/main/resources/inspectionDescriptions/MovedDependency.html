Reports Maven and Gradle dependencies whose publisher moved them to new coordinates.

A move comes from a <relocation> in the newest POM, for example mysql:mysql-connector-java to com.mysql:mysql-connector-j. Moves without relocation POMs come from a built-in list and are reported only when the new coordinates have a stable release: javax.* APIs to jakarta.*, Jackson 3 (tools.jackson), Testcontainers 2 modules, BouncyCastle *-jdk18on, AWS SDK v2, frozen libraries such as log4j:log4j and dom4j:dom4j, and the Gradle plugins com.github.johnrengelman.shadow and org.jetbrains.intellij.

The old coordinates may still get releases, as Jackson 2 does. Turn this inspection off if you stay on them on purpose.
